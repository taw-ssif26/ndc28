A house of light, since 1949

Notre Dame College was founded in November 1949 in Laxmibazar, Old Dhaka, by priests of the Congregation of Holy Cross, at the invitation of Archbishop Graner. In 1954 it moved to its present home in Motijheel and took the name Notre Dame — “Our Lady”.

Within a decade it topped the HSC merit list seven times in eight years. Its Debating Club (1953) pioneered organised debating in Bangladesh, and its clubs — science, rover, chess, theatre, humanitarian — shape character as much as grades.
